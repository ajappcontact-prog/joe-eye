Privacy policy

Your recordings are yours.

Last updated 20 October 2026

In short: Joe Eye keeps your recordings on your phone. It sends them somewhere only if you link your own Google Drive or Dropbox account. We collect no personal data, and we run no servers that receive your recordings.

Who we are

Joe Eye is an Android video recorder, published by Jonse of AJ Developer. It records with sound and keeps going with the screen off. While it records, Android always shows a notification. This policy covers the Joe Eye app and this website.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your recordings, in your phone’s DCIM folder (by default DCIM/MyRecorder/Videos). If you turn on Encrypt recordings, they are kept encrypted inside the app instead.
  • Notes and bookmarks you add while recording. They are kept inside the app, not in the gallery, and are not uploaded by cloud backup.
  • Your settings, such as camera, quality and theme.
  • An app-lock PIN, if you turn on App lock, kept only on the phone.
  • Your name, email address and Google account ID, only if you choose Sign in with Google (see below).
  • Cloud sign-in tokens, if you link a cloud account. They stay on the phone and are removed when you unlink.
  • A small debug log of app events and errors (see below).

None of this is sent to us.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera to record video, and to notice movement if you turn on motion-triggered recording. Movement is worked out on the phone from the live picture; those frames are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Microphone to record sound with your video. Nothing is kept except the recording itself.
  • Location (optional), only if you choose to draw the date, time and GPS position into your video. The position is saved inside the video on your phone and is not sent anywhere by the app.
  • Notifications to show that a recording is running, to give you Pause and Stop buttons, and to report problems.
  • Internet, used only for the optional cloud backup and Sign in with Google.
  • Biometrics, used only if you choose fingerprint or face unlock for App lock. The app never sees your fingerprint or face.
  • Battery optimization exemption (optional), so Android does not stop a long recording you started.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you tap Sign in with Google, Google asks you to share your name, email address and Google account ID with Joe Eye. They are stored only on your phone, to show who is signed in, and are never sent to us or to any server. Signing in is not required to use the app. Google handles the sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Google Drive

If you choose Link Google Drive, Joe Eye asks Google for the drive.file permission. This lets the app see and manage only the files and folders it created itself, in a folder named “Joe Eye”. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Drive.

Your recordings are uploaded directly from your phone to your Google Drive over HTTPS. They do not pass through any server operated by us. Joe Eye does not use your Google data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone.

Joe Eye’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Dropbox

If you choose Link Dropbox, Joe Eye signs in through Dropbox in your browser and is given an app folder (Apps/Joe Eye). The app can only see that folder, never the rest of your Dropbox. Recordings are uploaded directly from your phone to Dropbox over HTTPS. The app also reads the email address of the linked Dropbox account, to show which account is linked in Settings; it is kept only on your phone.

Storage figures and switching accounts

Once you link an account, Joe Eye asks Google Drive or Dropbox how much storage the account uses and has in total. It shows these figures in Settings and uses them on your phone to decide when new recordings switch to your other linked account. They are not sent to us or to any server.

Encryption (optional)

If you turn on Encrypt recordings, the app stores your recordings encrypted (AES-256) in its private storage, using a key held in the Android Keystore. They are hidden from the gallery and other apps. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, those recordings cannot be recovered. Cloud backup uploads a normal, readable copy.

Debug log (optional)

The app keeps a small log of app events, errors and crashes on your phone, with your phone model and Android version. It contains no video, audio, location or account details. It leaves your phone only when you tap Send debug log to the developer and press Send in your email app; it goes to aj.appcontact@gmail.com. You can clear it at any time in Settings.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service in the app. The only information that ever reaches us is what you choose to email us, such as a debug log.

This website

This site is a simple static page. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking scripts. Like any web host, our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and time of request, for security and operation.

How long data is kept

Recordings, notes, the optional profile and the debug log stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Emailed debug logs are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem.

Delete your account and data

Joe Eye has no server-side accounts. To delete everything it holds about you:

  1. In the app, open Settings > Account > Delete account data. This removes your name, email address and Google ID from the phone and clears the debug log.
  2. Delete any recordings you no longer want in the Library, or uninstall the app, which removes all app data.
  3. To stop Joe Eye having access to your cloud account, revoke it (see below). Files already uploaded to your own Drive or Dropbox stay there until you delete them.
  4. If you emailed a debug log and want it deleted, write to aj.appcontact@gmail.com.

Unlinking and revoking access

  • Unlink: in the app, go to Settings > Cloud backup and tap your account. Uploading stops and the app forgets your sign-in. Files already in your cloud account stay there until you delete them.
  • Revoke access: for Google, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. For Dropbox, visit dropbox.com/account/connected_apps.

If you turn on “Free up space after backup”, Joe Eye deletes each recording from your phone once its upload has been checked. Only do this if you are happy for your cloud copy to be the only one.

Recording other people

Laws about recording video, audio and location of other people differ by country. You are responsible for using the app lawfully and for getting any consent the law requires. Do not use Joe Eye to record people secretly.

Children

Joe Eye is not directed at children under 13 (or the age set by your country) and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
