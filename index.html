Features

Built for recording you don’t have to babysit.

01

Background recording

Start recording, turn the screen off and carry on. Joe Eye keeps going, and Android always shows a notification with Pause and Stop.

02

Motion trigger

Leave the camera watching and Joe Eye saves a separate clip each time something moves, then waits again. It uses more battery than a normal wait.

03

Widget, tile & shortcuts

Start and stop from a home-screen widget, the Quick Settings tile, or by holding the app icon. Both show whether you are recording, paused or waiting, and for how long.

04

Dual camera

Record with the front and back cameras at the same time on phones that support it, saved as two files.

05

Notes & bookmarks

While recording, tap Bookmark to mark a moment or Note to type a few words. They stay on your phone and are listed in the Library with their times.

06

Storage protection

When space runs low, Joe Eye saves what it has and stops cleanly before the disk fills. The storage dashboard shows how much is used and roughly how many more hours fit.

07

A Library that keeps up

Search, sort, name new recordings your own way, select several to share or delete, and see at a glance which ones are backed up.

08

App lock & encryption

Protect your Library and Settings with a PIN or fingerprint. Optionally encrypt recordings so they can only be played inside Joe Eye.

09

Optional cloud backup

Link your own Google Drive, Dropbox or both. Back up on Wi-Fi or while charging, send a single recording with one tap, and watch progress in the upload queue. With two accounts linked, new recordings move to the second one when the first reaches a limit you type, in percent or GB.

Cloud backup

Your account, your files, nothing in between.

  1. Tap Link Google Drive or Link Dropbox in the app’s Settings and sign in on the provider’s own page.
  2. Joe Eye gets a narrow permission. In Google Drive it can only see files it created itself, in a folder called “Joe Eye”. In Dropbox it gets its own app folder.
  3. Recordings upload straight from your phone to your account. They never pass through a server of ours, because we don’t have one.

You can unlink at any time in the app, or revoke access from your Google Account or Dropbox settings. If you turn on “Free up space after backup”, Joe Eye removes each recording from your phone only after its upload has been checked.

Questions

Quick answers.

Does Joe Eye collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or ads, and your recordings stay on your phone unless you link your own cloud account. Read the full privacy policy.

Does Joe Eye record in secret?

No. Android always shows a notification while Joe Eye records. You can make it quiet, but it is never hidden. Please only record where the law allows and with any consent required.

Can the app see my whole Google Drive?

No. It uses Google’s drive.file permission, which only covers files and folders that Joe Eye created itself.

Will my phone stop the recording in the background?

Some phones close background apps to save battery. Joe Eye has a guide in Settings for switching off battery optimization for it, and a health check that points out anything that could stop a recording.

Are my recordings encrypted?

Only if you turn on “Encrypt recordings”. They are then kept encrypted on your phone and can only be played in Joe Eye. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, encrypted recordings cannot be recovered.

Is it legal to record with the screen off?

Recording laws differ by country and situation, and some places require consent from the people you record. You are responsible for following them. See the terms.
